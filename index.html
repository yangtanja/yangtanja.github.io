The Studio

A carpet that takes off.

In Korean, yangtanja means a carpet — the kind that, in old tales, lifts off the ground and carries its rider somewhere new. That is what we try to make: a few quiet minutes that take you to a misty lake at dawn, a snowy station at midnight, or the quiet science behind an everyday thing.

YangTanJa is a small, independent studio. Everything we publish is written, directed and edited in-house, with one standard: calm craft, honest facts and pictures worth pausing on.

  • MusicOriginal instrumental and vocal works for rest, focus and memory.
  • StoriesNarrated life stories about family, second chances and small kindnesses.
  • KnowledgeShort films that explain the quiet science behind everyday things.

Principles

Three promises
in everything we make.

Music, stories or short films — whatever the form, the same three promises come first.

01

Calm craft

We make things slowly and carefully, so they feel unhurried to watch and listen to.

02

Honest facts

Every story and explanation is researched and checked before it reaches anyone.

03

Worth pausing on

Each frame and each note is chosen to be something you would want to stop and look at.

Craft

How a YangTanJa film is made.

  1. 01

    Research & Script

    Every episode starts with a researched, fact-checked script written for the ear.

  2. 02

    Voice

    Narration is produced and timed line by line, with subtitles in the viewer's language.

  3. 03

    Image

    Scenes are art-directed frame by frame for a consistent, cinematic look.

  4. 04

    Score

    Music from our own catalog or properly licensed libraries, mixed so the story comes first.

  5. 05

    Edit & Publish

    Final edits are reviewed by a person before anything goes live.

Transparency : some visuals, voices and music are created with the help of generative AI tools under human direction, and are labeled as such wherever a platform requires it.

Our Tool

YangTanJa Uploader

YangTanJa Uploader is a private, non-commercial desktop tool used only by the operator of the YangTanJa channels. It runs on the operator's own computer and uses the YouTube API Services to manage the operator's own channels.

  • Updates titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists of the operator's own videos.
  • Posts the operator's own comments on the operator's own videos.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics reports of the operator's own channels to create a private weekly report.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
