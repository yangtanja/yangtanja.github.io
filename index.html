The Studio

A carpet that takes off.

In Korean, yangtanja means a carpet — the kind that, in old tales, lifts off the ground and carries its rider somewhere new. That is what we try to make: a few quiet minutes that take you to a misty lake at dawn, a Showa-era station in the snow, or the reason a baseball curves.

YangTanJa is a small, independent studio. Every channel is written, directed and edited in-house, with a single standard across all of them: calm craft, honest facts and pictures worth pausing on.

  • MusicOriginal instrumental and vocal works for rest, focus and memory.
  • StoriesNarrated life stories about family, second chances and small kindnesses.
  • KnowledgeShort films that explain the science behind sports, cities and the human body.

Craft

How a YangTanJa film is made.

  1. 01

    Research & Script

    Every episode starts with a researched, fact-checked script written for the ear.

  2. 02

    Voice

    Narration is produced and timed line by line, with subtitles in the viewer's language.

  3. 03

    Image

    Scenes are art-directed frame by frame for a consistent, cinematic look.

  4. 04

    Score

    Original music from our own catalog, mixed so the story always comes first.

  5. 05

    Edit & Publish

    Final edits are reviewed by a person before anything goes live.

Transparency : some visuals, voices and music are created with the help of generative AI tools under human direction. These videos are labeled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.

Our Tool

YangTanJa Uploader

YangTanJa Uploader is a private, non-commercial desktop tool used only by the operator of the YangTanJa channels. It runs on the operator's own computer and uses the YouTube API Services to manage the operator's own channels.

  • Updates titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists of the operator's own videos.
  • Posts the operator's own comments on the operator's own videos.
  • Reads YouTube Analytics reports of the operator's own channels to create a private weekly report.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.
